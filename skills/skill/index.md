# All skills

> Every one of the 57 skills of agent-skills on its own page, generated from its SKILL.md: what it does, when to use it, how to call it, its scripts, the stacks it fits and its token cost.

57 skills are in development: 39 of them are in the released agent-skills 0.3.0, the other 18 ship in the next release. Each page is generated from the skill’s `SKILL.md`, so it says what the skill says.
